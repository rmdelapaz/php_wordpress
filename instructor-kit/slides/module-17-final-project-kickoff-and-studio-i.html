9.1 · The brief

What Maria needs

  • Reserve a book; kept three days; then expired
  • At most three waiting, no duplicates, no sold-out books; she changes the numbers
  • My holds: see and cancel your own, nobody else's. Ever.
  • A counter list, "collected"; an email with the last day
  • No JavaScript needed, screen readers, no reload; live prices
  • Personal data kept no longer than needed; tested; no Thursday deploys

9.1 · Requirement map

Which layer handles what

  • Reserve, three days, expired: Holds::place(), Holds::status()
  • My holds, only your own: HoldsTable::finish() with the user in the WHERE
  • Staff: Books > Holds, edit_others_posts
  • Email: pagesco_books_hold_placed + HoldMail
  • No JS: admin-post.php forms; with JS: the same form to REST
  • Personal data: deleted 30 days after pickup, dropped on uninstall
9.1 · Security and privacy

The checklist (20 points)

  • CSRF: a nonce per action and ID; nothing changes on GET
  • Access: read for customers, edit_others_posts for staff
  • IDOR: ownership in SQL; others' holds are "not found"
  • XSS: escape late; messages from a fixed list · SQL: prepare()
  • Open redirects, abuse limits, personal data on a schedule
PROOF The smoke test proves several lines from outside the code.
