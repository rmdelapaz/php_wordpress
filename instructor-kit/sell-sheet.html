Who It's For

Learners: PHP developers moving into WordPress, front-end developers and site builders ready to write themes and plugins. Prerequisites: PHP Foundations and MySQL Foundations or equivalent experience (PHP, SQL basics), plus HTML and CSS. Programs: trainers, bootcamps, colleges, corporate L&D, workforce programs; weekly, twice weekly or intensive.

Full program · 18 sessions · 54 h

Part 1 only · 8 sessions · 24 h

  • Expose REST endpoints, schedule cron, test, profile, package and deploy a site
